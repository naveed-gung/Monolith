# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We pledge to make participation in Monolith a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Standards
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Enforcement
Report unacceptable behaviour to **naveedsohailg@gmail.com**. Maintainers may
remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code.

Adapted from the Contributor Covenant, v2.1.
