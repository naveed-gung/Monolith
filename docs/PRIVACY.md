# Privacy

Monolith is offline-first and built around on-device ownership.

- **Your library stays on your device.** Downloads and imports live in the
  app's own folder; nothing is uploaded to the author.
- **No analytics or tracking** are bundled by the app.
- **Network use** is limited to fetching audio you request and checking GitHub
  for updates.
- **Permissions** (media library, storage) are used only to read/play your music.
